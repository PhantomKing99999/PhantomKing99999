## Hey, I'm Andy 👋

I build intelligent systems where **AI meets the physical world**. My work has ranged from human-to-robot motion retargeting and robotic-arm perception to generative models for medical imaging and molecular simulations for battery materials.

I'm a University of Chicago student in the Class of 2030, interested in the space between **machine learning, robotics, physics, and mathematics**. Away from a terminal, you can usually find me playing badminton, lifting, or reading.
